### Founders
23andMe was initially co-founded by Anne Wojcicki, Linda Avey, and Paul Cusenza, however when the company filed for bankruptcy, Anne Wojcicki claimed sole ownership by winning a $305 million bidding war through her organization the TTAM Research Institute.
